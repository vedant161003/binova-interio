Since 1958

Heritage of Precision

Architectural Silence

We believe that design should not shout. It should breathe. Binova Interio translates the rigor of Italian engineering into the intimacy of the home.

Origins in Assisi

The Evolution of Form

Rooted in the structural clarity of Assisi, our evolution has been a steady march towards reduction. We strip away the superfluous to reveal the essential geometry of living spaces, creating environments that serve as quiet backdrops to a life well-lived.

Material Honesty

The Art of the Joint

True luxury is found in the unseen details. It is in the seamless transition between stone and wood, the perfectly engineered hinge, and the absolute precision of every intersection. We honor materials by allowing their inherent qualities to dictate the form.

"Architecture is the learned game, correct and magnificent, of forms assembled in the light."
