Living Concepts

Domestic
Intimacy.

Architectural rigor applied to the living space. A continuous flow of volumes, where shelving and media units integrate seamlessly into the structural narrative of the home.

Bluna Living System

Spatial Flow

We approach the living area not as a container for objects, but as a volume of continuous space. Our wall systems are designed to eliminate visual barriers, extending architectural lines and fostering a sense of expansive calm. The absence of noise allows the geometry of the room to breathe.

Integrated Systems

Technology and storage should serve the space, not dominate it. TV units and shelving are conceived as intrinsic architectural elements, flush with walls or acting as structural dividers. Precision engineering ensures effortless operation hidden behind monolithic facades.

Organic Structure.

Bridging Japanese minimalism with Italian architectural rigor. Solid seasoned timber frameworks, mortise-and-tenon craftsmanship, and low-slung seating architectures invite tactile intimacy while preserving clean sightlines throughout the residence.
